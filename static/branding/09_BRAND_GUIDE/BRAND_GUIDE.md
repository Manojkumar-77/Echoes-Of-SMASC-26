# Echoes Of SMASC '26 — Premium Brand System

## Core identity
**Echoes Of SMASC '26**

Compact identity: **ES26**

Tagline: **CAPTURE • RELIVE • FOREVER**

Emotional line: **More Than Memories — A Lifetime Of Echoes.**

## Design direction
Luxury • cinematic • nostalgic • elegant • timeless

The supplied ES26 monogram is the primary identity. It must remain visually consistent across every device.

## Responsive logo rules
- 16–48px: icon-only / favicon
- 64–192px: compact logo
- 256–512px: app/admin identity
- 512px+: full master
- Desktop hero: master + wordmark
- Dark backgrounds: supplied gold/cream mark
- Light backgrounds: black/monochrome variant

## Brand colors
- Rich Black: #050507
- Charcoal: #131214
- Gold: #DAAF3F
- Gold Light: #FFDF84
- Cream: #F8F5E9
- Muted Gold Gray: #B4A991

## Typography
Luxury display: serif
UI/body: modern sans-serif
Keep letter spacing generous for the uppercase supporting text.

## Never
- Do not redesign the ES26 mark.
- Do not change the facial/subject content of photography when this logo is placed on photos.
- Do not stretch or skew the logo.
- Do not rotate it.
- Do not add random gradients or shadows to the master.
- Do not place it over busy detail without a quiet area.

## Package purpose
This is a complete responsive brand asset package, not a single image.
